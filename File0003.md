# Conclusion

## Setting an Intention of Continual Questioning

Ethical AI is an ongoing journey, not a one-time achievement. Professionals should involve communities in product development, build skills beyond technology, and understand how product decisions affect society and economic opportunities. Most importantly, make daily efforts to become stewards of a human-centered future. Our decisions today will shape generations to come. The goal is not just ethical AI, but an ethical society powered by AI.
