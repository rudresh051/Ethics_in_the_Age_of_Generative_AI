# Introduction

## Generative AI Ethics – The Urgency of Now

AI can enhance creativity, productivity, and equity, but it can also enable deepfakes, scams, and misinformation. Ethical AI does not happen automatically; it depends on human decisions and responsibility. As AI evolves, we must evaluate whether its applications promote human interests, dignity, justice, and equality. Always ask: Who benefits? Who might be harmed? Does it increase human agency? **Ethics must remain a human responsibility.**
