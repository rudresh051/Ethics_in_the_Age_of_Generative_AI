# Developing the skill of ethical analysis in AI

## Notes

### Generative AI Ethics – Responsible Technology

Generative AI offers significant opportunities across banking, agriculture, HR, and other industries by improving decision-making, productivity, and economic opportunities. However, irresponsible AI development can amplify existing biases, create deepfakes, spread misinformation, generate inaccurate advice, and raise copyright and ownership concerns.

**Vilas Dhar's Three-Pillar Ethical AI Framework:**

1. **Responsible Data Practices:** AI is only as ethical as its training data. Organizations must examine data sources, identify explicit and implicit biases, ensure diverse representation, and prevent historical discrimination.
2. **Well-Defined Boundaries:** Define the AI system's purpose, target users, intended use cases, and restrictions. Establish safeguards to prevent misuse and unintended applications.
3. **Robust Transparency:** Make AI inputs, outputs, recommendations, and decision-making processes traceable and auditable. Enable human oversight and stakeholder accountability.

**Real-World Application:** A company's chatbot generates offensive and inaccurate responses due to unfiltered internet training data and unrestricted conversations. The CTO takes it offline, replaces the dataset with cleaned customer interactions, removes personal information, performs bias detection, establishes conversation boundaries, introduces input-output checkpoints, and implements regular audits and real-time user feedback.

**Key Learnings:**
- Responsible AI must be considered from initial product design through deployment.
- Human oversight is essential for decisions affecting people's lives.
- Ethical AI requires data quality, privacy, fairness, transparency, and accountability.
- Understanding how users might misuse a system is as important as understanding its intended purpose.
- Preventing ethical issues during design is more effective than fixing them after deployment.

**Core Principle:** Building powerful AI is not enough. We must build AI that is fair, trustworthy, transparent, and aligned with human interests.
